### 1️⃣ Collapse

Collapse is used to show and hide content on the same page.

```
    Click Me
        ↓
    Content appears
        ↓
    Click Me
        ↓
    Content disappears
```
```

<button class="btn btn-primary"
        data-bs-toggle="collapse"
        data-bs-target="#content">
    Click Me
</button>

<div id="content" class="collapse">
    <p>This content will show and hide.</p>
</div>

```
